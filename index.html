How AutoFlow works

1. Upload a photo with + in the prompt box, or drop it anywhere on the canvas — it appears in the photo strip above the prompt box.
2. Drag the photo into the prompt box, or click it to attach it.
3. Write a simple prompt: what should happen in the video.
4. Tap the model chip to pick the model, ratio (16:9 / 9:16 / 1:1), resolution, length and how many variations (×1–×4).
5. Press → — the video is generated on Hugging Face and lands in All media, ready to play and download.

Without a photo, the prompt alone makes a text-to-video clip. Real generation needs a Hugging Face token in Settings (stored only in this browser). Without a token, nothing is generated and the app says so; if the account has no credits, it says “Needs credits” instead of faking a result.
